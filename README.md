# Ix Ink And Thread Studio

IX CONCEPTS LIMITED — PREMIUM WEBSITE DESIGN AND DEVELOPMENT



1. PROJECT OVERVIEW



Create a modern, premium, visually distinctive and fully responsive website for IX Concepts Limited, a printing, custom apparel and branding business based in Kampala, Uganda.



The business advertises services including:



- Custom T-shirt printing

- Hoodie and cap branding

- Corporate wear

- Security uniforms

- Embroidery

- Garment printing

- Promotional merchandise

- Personalised gifts and branded products



The website should communicate creativity, quality, professionalism and attention to detail.



The primary objective is to present the business professionally, showcase its products and services, and make it easy for potential customers to request quotations through WhatsApp.



Do not create a generic clothing e-commerce website. This is a custom printing, apparel branding and promotional merchandise business.



This is a flexible demonstration website. Where verified company information is unavailable, create clearly labelled editable placeholders or optional content sections rather than presenting unverified claims as facts. The site may include optional sections for company achievements, awards, history, founding story, milestones, capabilities and testimonials, but these must be marked as draft content or placeholders until approved by IX Concepts Limited.

Sand — #E4C9A7

Use for:

- Subtle decorative areas

- Supporting visual elements



Espresso — #3A332D

Use for:



- Headings

- Main text

Warm White — #FAF7F1

Use for:



- Main website background

- Content sections

- Service areas

- Clean negative space

- Light product-gallery backgrounds



Colour application rules



The website should primarily use warm white and espresso, with terracotta as the main accent and olive green as the secondary accent.



Sand should be used selectively to create warmth and visual separation between sections.



Do not use all five colours equally. Establish a clear visual hierarchy.



Avoid:



- Excessive gradients

- Neon colours

- Bright, unrelated blues

- Unnecessary rainbow colours

- Excessive shadows

- Overly decorative elements

- Too many competing accent colours



Maintain accessible text contrast, especially for buttons and small text. Use an appropriate darker terracotta or espresso text where necessary rather than placing low-contrast text on coloured backgrounds.



3. DESIGN DIRECTION



Create an aesthetic that combines:



- Modern editorial design

- Premium branding-studio presentation

- Clean product photography

- Contemporary typography

- Generous whitespace

- Structured layouts

- Strong visual hierarchy

- Subtle animations

- Carefully designed product cards

- Professional business presentation



The website should feel warm, distinctive, polished and memorable.



Use the supplied visual reference or visualization as the primary design direction. Recreate its overall composition, visual rhythm, spacing, image treatment, typography scale, card styling, animation feel and premium presentation as closely as possible while adapting it to IX Concepts Limited's brand palette and content. Do not copy another company's logo, text, proprietary assets or identity. If the reference includes visual effects, translate them into lightweight, responsive and accessible website interactions.



Avoid the appearance of a cheap template or an ordinary online clothing shop.



Use visual inspiration from excellent modern branding agencies, creative studios and premium product websites. Adapt relevant design principles without copying another company's identity.



4. TYPOGRAPHY



Use a bold, modern sans-serif typeface for headings and a highly readable sans-serif typeface for body text.



Suggested pairing:



- Headings: Manrope or Montserrat

- Body: Inter



Typography requirements:



- Strong and memorable homepage headline

- Clear heading hierarchy

- Comfortable body-text line spacing

- Readable mobile text sizes

- Consistent font weights

- Minimal use of all-capital text outside labels and short headings

10. REQUEST A QUOTE AND WHATSAPP INTEGRATION



Create a simple, attractive enquiry form that connects directly to WhatsApp.



Fields:



- Full name

- Phone number

- Product or service required

- Quantity

- Preferred colours

- Design or branding requirements

- Required completion date

- Additional information



Allow the customer to review the information before opening WhatsApp with a prepared message.



When the customer submits the form, generate a clear, URL-encoded WhatsApp message containing all completed fields and open a WhatsApp chat using the configured business number.



Use the WhatsApp click-to-chat format:



"https://wa.me/COUNTRYCODEPHONENUMBER?text=ENCODED_MESSAGE"



For the current configured number, use the international digits-only format for +256 759 606 713, while keeping the number stored in one clearly editable configuration value. Do not include the plus sign, spaces or punctuation in the "wa.me" number.



Include:



- A visible Send Enquiry on WhatsApp button

- A secondary direct WhatsApp button in the header or floating action area

- A clear notice that the customer must press Send in WhatsApp to submit the enquiry

- A fallback message if WhatsApp cannot be opened

- Validation so required fields are completed before the WhatsApp action is available

- Protection against silently discarding entered information



The WhatsApp number must remain configurable because the published business number must be confirmed before launch.



Do not require a backend or database for the initial version.

15. RESPONSIVE DESIGN



The website must work properly on:



- Mobile phones

- Tablets

- Laptops

- Desktop screens



Use a mobile-first approach.



Ensure:



- Images resize correctly.

- Navigation works on small screens.

- Buttons are comfortable to tap.

- Text does not overflow.

- Product grids adapt to screen size.

- Forms are easy to complete on mobile.

- Horizontal scrolling is avoided unless intentional.

- WhatsApp actions remain easy to find and use on mobile.

- Animations remain smooth on lower-powered devices.



16. ANIMATIONS AND INTERACTIONS



Use polished, high-quality animations inspired by the supplied visualization.



Include:



- Gentle fade-in and upward movement when sections enter the viewport

- Smooth hero image or background transitions

- Subtle image reveal effects

- Small button hover and press transitions

- Slight product-card movement or image scaling on hover

- Smooth gallery filtering and image enlargement

- Animated navigation indicators

- Smooth scrolling where appropriate

- Responsive mobile navigation

- A restrained floating WhatsApp button animation

- Loading and validation states for the quote form



Animations should feel premium, intentional and consistent with the reference visualization. Avoid excessive motion, distracting parallax, long delays or effects that reduce usability.



Respect "prefers-reduced-motion" preferences by disabling or simplifying non-essential animations.



All buttons, navigation items and forms must have meaningful functionality. Avoid decorative buttons that do nothing.

18. IMAGE REQUIREMENTS



Generate or source visuals that match the supplied visualization's overall art direction, composition, lighting, colour treatment, camera angle, product styling and premium editorial feel.



If suitable original images are unavailable, generate temporary visuals specifically for this demo based on the reference direction. Generated visuals may be used for general design presentation, hero sections, service illustrations and product concept cards, but they must not be presented as authentic completed IX Concepts Limited projects.



Image-generation guidance:



- Show premium branded apparel, printing, embroidery and promotional merchandise.

- Use warm, natural studio or editorial lighting.

- Reflect the terracotta, olive, sand, espresso and warm-white palette.

- Use clean compositions with generous negative space.

- Show realistic fabric texture, stitching, print detail and product quality.

- Avoid unrelated logos, fake client brands, watermarks and illegible text.

- Do not place invented company claims or customer identities in images.

- Create responsive crops suitable for desktop and mobile.

- Keep image assets easy to replace with approved business photography.



The preferred image sources are:



1. Original images supplied by IX Concepts Limited.

2. Images the business explicitly approves for website use.

3. Generated or licensed stock images used only as temporary visual placeholders.



Do not claim that stock images or AI-generated product images are actual work completed by the business.



Keep all placeholder images easy to replace.



Do not invent client brands or place unrelated logos on products.



19. SEO AND PERFORMANCE



Optimise the website for relevant searches involving:



- Custom T-shirt printing in Kampala

- Corporate wear in Kampala

- Embroidery and garment branding in Uganda

- Custom apparel

- Promotional merchandise in Uganda



Use these phrases naturally where accurate.

Optimise image sizes, use descriptive alternative text and maintain good mobile performance.



Include appropriate metadata for each page, including editable title and description fields.



20. FINAL DESIGN GOAL



The finished website should feel like a professionally designed branding studio's website, with a warm and distinctive visual identity.



The terracotta, olive, sand and espresso palette must be recognisable throughout the website without overwhelming the product photography.



The final result should be:



- Visually memorable

- Professional

- Easy to navigate

- Mobile-friendly

- Fast-loading

- Accessible

- Easy to maintain

- Focused on generating genuine customer enquiries

- Clearly connected to WhatsApp

- Faithful to the supplied visualization's design language

- Flexible enough to add verified company history, achievements, awards and milestones later



Prioritise design quality, authentic product presentation, a clear quotation process and a reliable WhatsApp enquiry experience.



Build the complete initial website experience, not merely a static hero section.
